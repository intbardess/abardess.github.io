Hello and Welcome!

My name is Integra Bardessono. I am a scientists, former teacher, and dabble in many artistic hobbies. This site is a personal and professional record: here you will find information about myself, my background, and some of the projects I have created throughout my life, including this website! I maintain this site myself and generally update it only once or twice a year, so it's a constant work in progress. That said, if you notice any critical errors feel free to reach out!

The site is NOT currently optimized for mobile!

A Brief History

I am a lifelong resident of Napa, California. I grew up loving math and science, and after attaining a physics degree went on to work in education. I was a teacher from 2021 to 2026, where I worked to provide opportunities for students to learn and ractice science in a safe and caring environment. I exited that career due to burn out but am still passionate about the work that I did. You can find more information under the teaching tab.

In my free time I enjoy yoga, swimming, video games, coding, and a lot of reading and writing. I have self-published a science fiction novel, and occasionally write reviews of the media (mostly books) I have enjoyed (or despised). I am a mostly self-taught computer programmer, and have so far completed two micro-games as part of a yearly 48 hour gamejam competition. I one day hope to publish a full-length video game.

Experience

Before I became a teacher, I spent over five years working with and helping to educate students at the high school and middle school levels; I worked on and off as a math tutor after I graduated from California Polytechnic State University in 2016, and I worked for three years as a camp instructor at a AstroCamp, VA, where I had the opportunity to provide for campers with a wide range of interests and needs. As a consequence, I gained a great deal of experience developing curricula and presenting science concepts to a large range of learners, from 4th grade up through high school. After my time at AstroCamp, I decided to become a teacher, and in 2022 I graduated from Sonoma State University with credentials in physics and chemistry.

Professional Development & Certifications

In addition to my science credentials, I completed a Maker Eucation Certification through Sonoma State University over the course of the 23-24 academic year. I also added a general Computer Science authorization to my credeitial in 2025, also through SSU. More information can be found here.

I was certified to teach AP Computer Science Principles through College Board in 2024.

The Writing Revolution is a an organization and book advocating and developing resources for increased literacy and writing in the classroom. I found their course incredibly valuable, and their materials immediately useful. I recieved a certification for completing their PD session in late 2023.
